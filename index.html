What are X39 and X49?

X39 supports improved exercise performance, improvements in strength and stamina, energy flow, and overall health and well-being. X49 promotes performance, strength, and stamina; reduces recovery time following exercise; and supports maintaining a healthy cardiovascular system through exercise and a healthy weight through diet and exercise programs. Both use patented phototherapy technology and contain no drugs or stimulants.

How do LifeWave phototherapy patches work?

Your body emits heat, including heat in the infrared spectrum. LifeWave patches are designed to trap this infrared energy when placed on the body, causing them to reflect it back to stimulate specific points on the skin that can promote a general state of health and healthy activity unique to each LifeWave patch.

What's included in the Performance Pack?

The X39 + X49 Performance Pack includes 1 sleeve of X39 (30 patches) and 1 sleeve of X49 (30 patches) — 60 patches total.

How do I use X39 and X49?

Place one X39 or X49 patch on the body, preferably behind the neck and under the belly button. Apply the patch to clean, dry skin in the morning. Patches may be worn for up to 12 hours before discarding. Keep well hydrated while using this product.
